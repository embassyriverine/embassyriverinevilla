## 1. Introduction

Embassy Riverine is a low-density independent villa community located in Tharahunise (also spelled Tarahunise), North Yelahanka, Bengaluru. It forms the exclusive villa precinct within the larger Embassy Origins masterplan and is designed around water systems, ecological corridors and a sense of openness.

This short guide brings together practical information on location, layout philosophy, villa character, pricing context and important points for buyers and investors. It is intended as a clear starting reference rather than a substitute for official documentation or a site visit.

The project is registered under Karnataka RERA (**PRM/KA/RERA/1251/309/PR/090926/008924**). Possession is currently indicated around September 2032. For the most current availability, floor plans and pricing, the primary source is the official project page.

---

## 2. Location & Setting

Embassy Riverine sits on Chapparkallu Road in Tharahunise Village, Jala Hobli, North Yelahanka. The site is near Tarahunise Lake and benefits from access via NH44 / Bellary Road. Kempegowda International Airport is approximately 20 minutes away under normal traffic conditions.

The wider area forms part of the North Bangalore growth corridor. While civic and social infrastructure continues to develop, the location offers a quieter residential character compared with denser city nodes. Buyers should map schools, healthcare, daily needs and road conditions as part of their evaluation.

The villa community is positioned inside the broader Embassy Origins township, which also includes an apartment component and has stated plans for further residential and commercial phases on adjoining land. This integrated context is relevant both for future amenities and for understanding how the neighbourhood may evolve.

---

## 3. Layout & Design Philosophy

The defining idea of Embassy Riverine is a landscape-led, water-centric masterplan. A riparian water corridor runs through the precinct. Every villa home is positioned along one of six ecological corridors:

- Flowering  
- Fragrant  
- Open grass  
- Edible  
- Active  
- Shaded  

Planting is multi-layered and intended to create shade, greenery and a cooler microclimate. Backyards are designed to dissolve into the landscape and water rather than ending at hard boundary walls. This produces a continuous green and water spine instead of isolated plots separated by high walls.

The villa precinct occupies roughly **50–55 acres** and contains **217 Villa Homes**. This low density relative to land area is central to the project’s independent-living positioning. The larger first-phase residential area of Embassy Origins is reported at approximately 85 acres.

---

## 4. Villa Configurations & Community Character

Configurations are primarily **4, 4.5 and 5 BHK** independent villas. Built-up areas generally range from approximately **4,250 sq ft to over 7,300 sq ft** depending on the collection. Homes are arranged to support indoor-outdoor living, with private gardens and direct or visual connection to the corridors and water elements.

The community has its own clubhouse and amenity set, kept distinct from the apartment side of the township. Reported amenities include outdoor pools, lawns, picnic areas, pet parks, sports facilities and children’s zones. The overall character emphasises privacy, restoration and connection to landscape.

## 5. Pricing Context & Practical Notes

Pricing for Embassy Riverine villas has been reported in a broad ultra-premium band. Figures circulating in the market and listings have ranged from the low-to-mid teens (crores) upward depending on size, orientation and corridor adjacency. Exact quotes vary and should always be confirmed directly from official channels, as preferred orientations and remaining inventory can influence the numbers offered.

Because the total number of villas is finite (**217**), scarcity dynamics can affect both choice and relative pricing over time. Early movers typically have wider selection of orientations and garden/corridor relationships. Buyers should request current unit-wise quotes, understand the construction-linked payment schedule, and factor in maintenance expectations for the extensive landscape and water systems.

Investment considerations include the limited supply of comparable independent waterfront-style villas in the corridor, airport proximity, and the longer-term township vision. These should be balanced against execution timelines, infrastructure readiness in Tharahunise, and personal liquidity and holding-period requirements.

## 6. Key Considerations Before Proceeding

- Verify the latest RERA documents, sanctioned plans and possession timelines.
- Visit the site or Discovery Centre to experience the scale of the corridors and the relationship between homes and water.
- Compare specific plot orientations, garden sizes and corridor types — these differ meaningfully across the community.
- Obtain written quotes and payment schedules rather than relying solely on indicative ranges.
- Assess surrounding infrastructure (roads, water, schools, healthcare) against your daily needs.
- Factor total cost of ownership, including long-term maintenance of landscape and common areas.
- Align the decision with your intended use (primary residence vs investment) and realistic holding period.
